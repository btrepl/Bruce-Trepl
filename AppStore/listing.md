## Description
How far can YOU hit it?

Pick a slugger, step up to the plate, and smash a baseball as far as you can — measured in feet. Pick a season to decorate the field: Fall pumpkins on the ground, Christmas lights dangling, or a plain field.

Choose from Santa, the Ballplayer, Bigfoot, a funny Snowman, Princess Ruby, and Indiana Jane — each with a signature bat, from a roll of gift wrap to a royal scepter. Or build your own slugger and your own bat and take them to the plate.

Time your swing, watch it fly, and climb the leaderboard. Practice as much as you want for free, or play Ranked — 10 ranked swings a day — to climb the board and earn coins.

Features:
• 6 hilarious sluggers with signature bats
• Build-a-Slugger and Build-a-Bat workshops
• 3 seasons: Fall (pumpkins on the ground), Christmas (lights dangling), Normal (plain field)
• Funny sayings for every hit — hype for moonshots, roasts for dribblers
• Unlimited practice swings + 10 ranked swings a day + leaderboard
• Friends leaderboard — see how your friends did, and challenge them to beat your best hit
