# Privacy Policy — How Far Can You Hit It?

Last updated: October 27, 2026

## Overview
"How Far Can You Hit It?" is a casual batting game. This policy explains what information the game does and does not collect.

## Information we collect
**None.** The developer does not collect, store, or share any personal information from players.

## Information stored on your device
Game progress — including your slugger and bat selections, daily swing count, and local high scores — is stored only on your own device. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.

## Purchases
Extra swings and custom characters/bats are sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payments are processed by Apple, and the developer never sees or stores your payment information.

## Leaderboards
If you use the global leaderboard, it runs through Apple's Game Center. Your Game Center nickname and scores are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

## Children
The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Advertising and tracking
The game contains no third-party advertising and no tracking tools.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
